Ask an expert
What is spunbond?

A nonwoven fabric made from continuous polypropylene fibres bonded together with heat: light, even and strong.

What does gsm mean?

The weight of one square metre of fabric in grams. A higher gsm means thicker, stronger fabric.

Hydrophilic or hydrophobic?

Hydrophobic for bags and covers; hydrophilic is usually better for hygiene and disposable products.
