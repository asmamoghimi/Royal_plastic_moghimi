Choosing a film and additives

Not sure what to order? These common combinations are a good starting point. Click an item to read more.

01

General agricultural cover

For crop covers and everyday use: 80 to 150 microns, 1 to 3 layers, width to suit the field (usually 2 to 8 m). Add UV if it is used outdoors.

02

Long-term outdoor use

For several seasons in the sun: 150 to 250 microns, 3 to 5 layers, with UV and IR if needed.

03

Light greenhouse

For lighter greenhouses and normal use: 120 to 180 microns, 3 layers or more, with UV and anti-drip if needed.

04

Heavy-duty greenhouse

For large roofs and long use: 180 to 250 microns, 3 to 5 layers, with UV, anti-drip and IR if needed.

05

UV

The most important additive for any cover in the sun. Without it, even thick film soon turns brittle, so choose the right additive first and the thickness second.

06

Anti-drip

Stops drops collecting and falling on plants; the cover stays cleaner and the risk of disease is lower.

07

IR (heat retention)

Keeps heat inside the greenhouse at night. If temperature control matters to you, IR usually does more than adding thickness for no reason.

08

Anti-static

Reduces surface static so less dust settles and more light passes through.

09

Coloured and custom

We produce the colour, thickness, width and additive mix exactly to your order.

From granules to rolls

How greenhouse and agricultural film is made at the Royal Plastic Moghimi factory.

01 Blending material and additives

Polyethylene is blended with the ordered additives (UV, anti-drip, IR, anti-static or colour).

02 Multi-layer extrusion

1 to 5 layers are extruded and joined at the same time.

03 Blowing and cooling

The film is blown into a large bubble and cooled with air.

04 Thickness control

Thickness is controlled across the full width so the film is even and sits properly on the frame.

05 Folding and winding

The wide film is folded and wound onto rolls at the ordered length.

06 Quality control and packing

Thickness, width, length and roll weight are checked against the order and packed.

Ask an expert
Does a greenhouse cover need UV?

If it is in the sun, yes. UV stops the film turning brittle early and makes the cover last longer.

Does thicker film last longer?

Up to a point. But if the film needs UV and does not have it, extra thickness will not help. Adding thickness for no reason only adds weight and cost; thickness and additives should be chosen together.

What exactly does anti-drip do?

It stops condensation collecting on the roof and dripping on the plants, and keeps the cover cleaner.

What is IR for?

It keeps heat inside the greenhouse at night. Recommended for cold regions or whenever temperature control matters.

What is the benefit of multiple layers?

More resistance to wind, stretching and tearing, especially for long use and harsh conditions.

What if I do not know what to order?

Tell us the size of the greenhouse or field, the region and how long you will use it. We will suggest the right thickness, layers and additives so you do not pay for more than you need.
