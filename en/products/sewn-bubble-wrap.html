Why sewn bubble bags?

Ready

Faster packing

The bag or cover is ready to use: just put the product in. Packing time drops and there is no cutting waste.

Exactly your size

Each product gets a bag or cover made to its own dimensions.

Shock protection

The same air bubbles as bubble wrap keep goods safe in transit.

Moisture and dust protection

The product stays protected from moisture and dust inside the bag.

Reusable

The bags can be used several times.

From roll to bag

How sewn bubble bags are made at the Royal Plastic Moghimi factory.

01 Making the bubble wrap

First, a roll of two or three-layer bubble wrap is produced.

02 Cutting to size

The roll is cut to the ordered bag or cover dimensions.

03 Heat sealing

The edges are sealed together with heat.

04 Flap and closure

A fold-over flap, adhesive strip or zip is added when ordered.

05 Counting and packing

Bags are counted and packed in fixed quantities.

06 Quality control

Seals and dimensions are checked and the order is shipped.
