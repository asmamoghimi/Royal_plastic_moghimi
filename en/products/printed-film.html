From design to roll

How printed film is made at the Royal Plastic Moghimi factory.

01 Receiving the design

Your design and logo are checked and prepared for printing.

02 Making the plates

A printing plate is made for each colour.

03 Producing the film

Polyethylene film is produced at the ordered thickness and width.

04 Printing

The design is printed on the film on a flexo press.

05 Cutting and forming

The film is cut into rolls or bags.

06 Quality control and packing

Print quality and colours are checked and the product is packed.

Ask an expert
How many colours can you print?

Up to 6. Fewer colours mean lower plate costs.

Do we need to supply the design?

It is best to send your logo and design files; we will help you prepare them for print.

Are plates charged separately?

Yes. Plates are made once and reused for later orders.

What is the minimum order?

Printed film has a minimum order. Contact us for quantities and prices.

What is the lead time?
