Types of stretch film

Thickness, production method and additives decide each roll’s holding force, clarity and cost. Click a type to read more.

01

Hand

Narrow rolls for hand dispensers; suited to small numbers of pallets and irregular or mixed loads in the warehouse.

02

Machine

Wider, stronger rolls for automatic and semi-automatic wrappers on high-volume lines.

03

Cast

Made by cast extrusion: clear, quiet when unwinding, with high cling and even thickness. Our most common general-purpose grade.

04

Blown

Made by blown extrusion: more puncture resistance and higher holding force for heavy or sharp-edged loads.

05

Pre-stretched

Mechanically stretched during production; it needs less force to wrap and each roll covers more pallets.

06

Coloured

In custom colours for brand identification, hiding contents or colour-coding warehouse zones.

07

Black / security

Fully opaque; hides the pallet contents. Suited to valuable goods and sensitive export shipments.

08

Perforated

With small holes that let moisture and gas escape; suited to agricultural produce and loads that need to breathe.

09

UV-resistant

Formulated to resist sunlight for loads that stay outdoors for a long time.

10

Stretch hood

A wrap-free alternative: a large elastic hood is pulled down over the whole pallet in one movement.

11

Custom

We produce any combination of thickness, width, colour and cling so the film is made for your load and machine.

Applications

Logistics and pallet handling

  • Securing cartons and sacks on pallets for storage.
  • Keeping mixed pallets stable during forklift handling and racking.
  • Reducing load shift and product damage in road transport.
  • Protecting pallets from dust and moisture in open storage.

Manufacturing and industry

  • Bundling raw materials, coils and profiles for internal transfer.
  • Wrapping finished goods before they leave the line.
  • Protecting metal and machined parts from scratches and moisture.
  • Securing irregular or large industrial loads.

Building materials

  • Wrapping bricks, tiles and bagged cement on pallets.
  • Bundling pipes, rebar and timber for site delivery.
  • Protecting materials from rain and dust in storage.
  • Securing loads on flatbed trucks.

Home appliances and electronics

  • Wrapping appliance cartons on pallets for delivery to stores.
  • Keeping housings free of scratches and dust.
  • Bundling small cartons for easier handling.
  • Protecting against moisture in distribution warehouses.

Furniture

  • Wrapping sofas and chairs to protect fabric and upholstery.
  • Preventing scratches on wood and metal surfaces in transit.
  • Holding parts and doors closed on the road.
  • Protecting against dust in storage and showrooms.

Automotive parts

  • Wrapping spare parts on pallets for dealers.
  • Bundling metal and plastic parts for transfer between lines.
  • Protecting painted parts from scratches and dust.
  • Securing loads in containers for export.

From resin to roll

A step-by-step look at how stretch film is made at the Royal Plastic Moghimi factory.

01 Resin blending

LLDPE resin is blended with cling, colour or UV additives before entering the extruder.

02 Cast extrusion

The molten resin passes through a flat die and forms a continuous multi-layer film on a chill roll.

03 Cooling and thickness control

The film is cooled quickly and its thickness is monitored in real time across the full width.

04 Slitting to width

The master roll is slit to standard widths of 10 to 300 cm or to a custom width.

05 Pre-stretching and winding

The film is wound onto cores under controlled tension; pre-stretched grades are stretched at this stage.

06 Quality control and packing

Rolls are inspected for cling, clarity and even thickness and packed for shipping.

Ask an expert
What is stretch film made of?

Our stretch film is made from linear low-density polyethylene (LLDPE), a polymer with high elongation, puncture resistance and natural cling.

What is the difference between hand and machine stretch film?

Hand film comes in narrower rolls for hand dispensers and small or irregular pallet runs; machine film is wider and stronger, made for automatic wrappers on high-volume lines.

Which thickness should I choose?

It depends on the weight and sharpness of the load: light, even loads are well protected by thinner film of about 7 to 15 microns, and heavy or irregular loads by thicker film up to 40 microns.

What widths and roll weights are available?

Widths from 10 to 300 cm, roll weights from 0.5 to 60 kg depending on width and length, and 1, 2 or 3-inch cores.

What is the difference between cast and blown film?

Cast film is clearer, quieter when unwinding and more economical for general use; blown film has more puncture resistance and higher holding force for heavy or sharp-edged loads.

Do you make coloured stretch film?

Yes. Clear film is standard, and we also make coloured, opaque and black versions for branding, hiding contents or colour-coding.

Is stretch film recyclable?

Yes. LLDPE stretch film can be recycled in plastic film recycling schemes (resin code 4), and we also offer thinner grades that use less material.

Do you supply for export?

Yes. We produce at our factory in Iran and support international buyers through our Europe office in Amsterdam, with export packing and a production capacity of 1,000 tons a month.
