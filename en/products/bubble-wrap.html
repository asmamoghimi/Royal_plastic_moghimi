Types of bubble wrap

Each type is made for a specific job. Click a type to read more.

01

Two-layer

One bubble layer and one flat layer. The most economical choice for light goods, scratch protection and general packaging. For heavy goods or rough transport, three-layer is safer.

02

Three-layer

The bubbles sit between two flat layers, so they burst less easily and absorb impact better. A balanced choice for sensitive goods, secure packaging and light to medium industrial use.

03

Small bubble

Small bubbles give more even contact with the product. Suited to small, delicate parts, decorative goods and surfaces that must not be scratched.

04

Medium bubble

A balance between surface protection and shock absorption; the most popular choice for general packaging and shipping.

05

Large bubble

Large bubbles up to 30 mm absorb more impact and fill empty space in the carton. Suited to heavier and bulky goods.

06

Anti-static

For packing circuit boards, components and electronic devices that are sensitive to static electricity.

07

Metallised

A shiny metallised layer that reflects radiant heat. Mostly used as light thermal insulation in the roofs and walls of portable cabins, sheds and light structures.

08

Coloured

Bubble wrap in different colours for better-looking packaging, branding or colour-coding goods.

09

Printed

Your logo and design printed on bubble wrap; a dedicated Royal Plastic service.

From granules to rolls

How bubble wrap is made at the Royal Plastic Moghimi factory.

01 Material preparation

Polyethylene granules are prepared, with colour or anti-static additives when ordered.

02 Melting and extrusion

The material is melted and extruded as film layers.

03 Forming the bubbles

One layer is drawn by vacuum over a patterned roller to form bubbles of the ordered size.

04 Laminating the layers

The flat layer (and in three-layer, a third layer) is sealed onto the bubbles to trap the air.

05 Printing, slitting and winding

Printed if ordered, cut to the ordered width and length, and wound into rolls.

06 Quality control and packing

Bubbles, layer seals and dimensions are checked, and rolls are packed for shipping.

Ask an expert
Should I buy two-layer or three-layer?

For light goods, surface protection and cost control, two-layer is enough. If the goods are heavier or the route is long and rough, three-layer is the safer choice.

Are small or large bubbles better?

For delicate goods and sensitive surfaces, small bubbles (10 to 15 mm) work better. When shock absorption or void fill matters more, medium or large bubbles perform better.

What is the widest roll you produce?

Up to 230 cm; Royal Plastic is one of the few manufacturers in Iran with this width. The narrowest is 10 cm.

How long are the rolls?

From 10 to 1,000 m, depending on the width and your needs.

What is anti-static bubble wrap for?

For packing boards and electronic components that can be damaged by static electricity.

What is metallised bubble wrap used for?

Mostly as light thermal insulation in the roofs and walls of cabins, sheds and light structures. For normal packaging, two-layer or three-layer is a better fit.

Can you print our logo on the wrap?

Yes. Printing your brand and design on bubble wrap is one of Royal Plastic’s dedicated services.
