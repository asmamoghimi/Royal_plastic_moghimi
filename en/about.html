A family business since 1996

Royal Plastic Moghimi is a family business that has been producing plastic and packaging films since 1996, and is a well-known name in the industry in Iran.

Today we produce bubble wrap and sewn bubble bags, greenhouse and agricultural film, stretch film, shrink film and printed film, and we supply spunbond, for both the domestic market and export.

Most of our customers have worked with us for years. Our factory doors are open to customers, and you are welcome to see the production line for yourself.
